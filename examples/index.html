What the app does

Explore
Search by place name and coordinates, fly to places, clicked features, places nearby
Layers
2D and 3D (terrain, buildings, sky), map and aerial photos, layer toggles, legend
Draw
Points, lines, polygons and circles, editing, snapping, split and merge
Measure
Distances and areas on the ellipsoid, elevation profiles
Analyze
A choropleth of Voronoi cells with its legend, routing on roads, line of sight and viewsheds
Data
Reading and writing CSV, Shapefile, KML, GPX and more, an attribute table and computed columns
The maps read GSI's tiles directly (source: 国土地理院ベクトルタイル提供実験, 国土地理院 標高タイル). With ?local=1 they read the z14 tiles around Tokyo Station fetched for the benchmarks (bench/fixtures).
